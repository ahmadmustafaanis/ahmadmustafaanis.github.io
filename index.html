About

Building visual systems that learn from unlabeled data.

I'm an M.S. student in AI Engineering at CMU. Previously, I built computer-vision systems at Roll.ai, completed the Fatima Fellowship, and earned my B.S. in Computer Science from IIUI in Islamabad.
